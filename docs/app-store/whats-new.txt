- Images, stacks and EBSD master patterns keep their file's bit depth, so large files open in far less memory, and closing a file gives its memory back.
- Particles can analyse a region you draw; the FFT transform exports as shown; a Settings window chooses what the scroll wheel does (Zoom or Pan).
- Edit > Undo puts back a removed mark, spot, ring or EELS reference.
- Bruker, EDAX and EMsoft .h5 orientation maps open in the Maps room; damaged files are refused in words instead of stopping the app.
- Results change: EDS absorption on a tilted GMS file takes the tilt from the file, as exSpy does; grain sizes on maps with unequal x and y steps use both steps; colour maps follow matplotlib's own tables.
