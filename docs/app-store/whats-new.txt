- Diffraction simulation: take a phase from the Materials Project or a CIF and see its spot pattern over the measured one, and its crystal in 3D.
- Index finds the full orientation of a spot pattern.
- Tilt planning: a zone-axis map and the α and β that bring a zone axis on, with the holder's tilt axis and β sense calibrated from two patterns.
- Precipitate variants over the matrix, powder rings and X-ray patterns, and Find Phases to rank which phase explains the rings.
- HRTEM: a slab from a phase, an image with live defocus and thickness, beside the measured one.
- CBED with a thickness series, STEM images with detector bands, atom models from XYZ and POSCAR files, thickness from a two-beam CBED disc, and strain maps from a lattice image.
- Export Figure… writes vector figures; HyperSpy files, CIF and a methods record go out too.
