# Electron Microscopy Studio 2.6.1

Fixes for diffraction, simulation documents and the EDS energy axis.

**Fixed**
- **EDS energy axis.** The axis is refined from 0.6 keV up, so the carbon and oxygen K lines no longer tilt the gain. Peak positions and the elements found can move slightly on spectra with strong light-element lines.
- **Diffraction.** Spots that no simulated reflection explains are drawn dashed. Exported figures place their labels where the room shows them.
- **Simulation documents.** They show the Dynamical beams, their spots table lists what the screen shows, and they keep the hkl labels setting.
- **Add Phase.** Matches are written as GaAs, not AsGa, and each one names its space group, so zincblende and wurtzite can be told apart before you click.
- **Image room.** The selected profile's chart and the found objects, with their counts, sit under the image as tabs beside Output. Figure…, Profile CSV… and Particles CSV… are one Export section, last in the inspector.
- **Every room.** The Results room's two tables are split by a divider you can drag, like the other rooms. The Output log stays closed until you open it from View > Show Output. Histograms read min, mean and max, with the window's edges on a line of their own.

**Results change** for EDS: the refined energy axis can move fitted line positions on spectra with strong carbon or oxygen lines.

Requires macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
