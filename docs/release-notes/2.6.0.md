# Electron Microscopy Studio 2.6.0

Simulate electron diffraction, HRTEM, CBED and STEM images, index a spot pattern, and put figures straight into a paper.

**New**
- **Index** finds the spots of a diffraction pattern and its full crystal orientation. Drag the simulated pattern to tilt it, step through the solutions, flip it, fit the scale.
- **Phases** from the Materials Project or a CIF; a phase you added once works offline, and Save as CIF writes it out. Find Phases ranks them against your pattern and the elements of your EDS map.
- **Simulation documents**: File > New Simulation shows a crystal's diffraction pattern with the crystal beside it. Save it as a small `.emsim` file and open it again later.
- **HRTEM, CBED and STEM simulation**: a live HRTEM image with defocus, thickness, optics and frozen phonons; convergent-beam patterns with a thickness series and two-beam thickness measurement; STEM images with BF, ABF, ADF and HAADF bands. Place a simulation beside your measured image, or export it as a script.
- **Diffraction tools**: simulated spots over your pattern for any zone axis (with double diffraction), a spots table, a holder tilt planner, powder patterns and precipitate variants. A pattern saved as PNG or JPEG opens too.
- **Figures and files**: Export Figure writes a PDF or SVG with live text; Copy Figure pastes into Keynote; HyperSpy files go out; every export carries a record of the method and settings that made it.
- Quick Look for DigitalMicrograph, MRC, Velox and TIA files in the Finder; TIA `.ser` and `.emi` files open.
- Strain maps from a lattice image, keys to move between rooms, a welcome window with tutorials, a Help menu, and demo data for a spot pattern, silicon and an EELS core loss.
- An AI assistant you connect can work every room and open files from a folder you choose. Off by default.

**Improved**
- Diffraction is laid out in steps (Calibrate, Index, Simulate, Tilt Plan, Powder, Thickness); an indexed pattern labels only its strongest spots, so it stays readable.
- Toolbar: Simulate for simulations, Export for every room. EBSD Maps shows its results beside the map.
- Faster EBSD dictionary indexing and pattern filtering.
- Numbers read the same way everywhere, and variants and warnings no longer rely on colour alone.

**Fixed**
- A HyperSpy spectrum saved as `.hdf5` opens.
- Updating the downloaded app renames it to Electron Microscopy Studio.

**Results change** in two places: a ring calibration now fits the central background as py4DSTEM does (the Au standard moves by about 0.2 %); and SEM-EDS files (Bruker, EDAX, JEOL) give line intensities only, as eXSpy does, with no wt% or at%.

Requires macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
