# Electron Microscopy Studio 2.6.0

The biggest release so far: simulate electron diffraction, HRTEM, CBED and STEM images, index a spot pattern, and put
figures straight into a paper.

**New**
- **Index**: finds the spots of a diffraction pattern and its full crystal orientation. Drag the simulated pattern to tilt it, step through the solutions, flip it, fit the scale.
- **Phases**: search the Materials Project or open a CIF; a phase you added once works offline. Find Phases ranks them against your pattern and the elements of your EDS map.
- **Simulation documents**: File > New Simulation shows the diffraction pattern of a crystal with the crystal beside it. Save it as a small `.emsim` file and open it again later.
- **HRTEM, CBED and STEM simulation**: a live HRTEM image with defocus, thickness, optics and frozen phonons; convergent-beam patterns with a thickness series and two-beam thickness measurement; STEM images with BF, ABF, ADF and HAADF detectors. Show a simulation beside your measured image, or export it as a script.
- **Diffraction tools**: simulated spots over your pattern for any zone axis (with double diffraction), a spots table with hkl and d, a holder tilt planner, powder patterns and precipitate variants. A pattern saved as PNG or JPEG opens too.
- **Figures and files**: Export Figure writes a PDF or SVG with live text, and Copy Figure pastes into Keynote. Export HyperSpy files. Every export carries a record of the method and settings that made it.
- **Quick Look** for DigitalMicrograph, MRC, Velox and TIA files in the Finder, and TIA `.ser` and `.emi` files open.
- Strain maps from a lattice image, keys to move between rooms, a welcome window with recent files and tutorials, a Help menu, and demo data for a spot pattern and a silicon structure.
- An AI assistant you connect can work every room and open files from a folder you choose. Off by default.

**Improved**
- Diffraction is laid out in steps (Calibrate, Index, Simulate, Tilt Plan, Powder, Thickness); indexed patterns label only the strongest spots, so they stay readable.
- The toolbar has Simulate for simulations and Export for every room.
- Faster EBSD dictionary indexing and pattern filtering.
- Numbers read the same way everywhere, switches replace doubled check boxes, and variants and warnings no longer rely on colour alone.

**Fixed**
- A HyperSpy spectrum saved as `.hdf5` opens.
- Updating the downloaded app renames it to Electron Microscopy Studio.

No results change from the previous version.

Requires macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
